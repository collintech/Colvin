Owner-operated staging utility

Encrypted backups for Colvin staging.

Colvin Scheduled Backups is a private operational utility maintained by collintech for the Colvin vehicle-intelligence project. It is designed to copy encrypted PostgreSQL staging backups to the operator's own Google Drive account on a schedule.

The scheduled service is being configured. Unattended backup and recovery acceptance is pending a successful cloud run, an isolated restore and monitoring validation.

What the utility does

  • Reads a consistent database snapshot through a dedicated read-only connection.
  • Encrypts the export with the operator's public recovery key before uploading it.
  • Uploads the encrypted archive, checksum and completion receipt to a private Drive folder.
  • Reads the encrypted archive back to verify its checksum and records backup completion.
  • Supports retention of completed snapshots and a success check-in to the backup monitor.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The utility requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file to create and manage its backup files and folders. This scope limits access to files created by the app or explicitly authorized for it. The worker is configured to use a dedicated scheduled-backup folder. Google authorization is granted by the operator through rclone on the operator's computer.

Railway hosts the scheduled worker; Google Drive stores the encrypted outputs. The private recovery key and its passphrase stay under the operator's control for local recovery.

Data practices and support

Read the privacy policy for access, storage, retention and revocation details, and the terms of service for authorized use. General questions can be raised in the Colvin repository. Keep credentials, recovery material and database archives out of public issues.
