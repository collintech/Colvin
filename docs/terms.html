Purpose and authorized use

Colvin Scheduled Backups is maintained by collintech for authorized backup and recovery operations on Colvin staging data. Use is limited to an operator who controls, or has permission to access, the source database and destination Google Drive account.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for authorizing the intended account, maintaining an owner-only destination, protecting credentials and recovery material, monitoring backup freshness and reviewing retention settings. The private recovery key must remain available for as long as retained archives require it.

Backup and recovery limits

A successful encrypted upload and checksum comparison establish transfer integrity. Recovery must also be tested by restoring an archive in an isolated environment. Schedules can be delayed or interrupted by service outages, expired or revoked access, storage limits and other operational failures. The utility does not guarantee uninterrupted operation or recovery of every change. The operator remains responsible for additional copies and recovery testing appropriate to the data.

Retention and stopping the utility

Enabling automatic retention permits removal of expired, recognized backups within the configured scheduled-backup folder. The operator should review candidates before enabling deletion. The worker can be disabled, its Google authorization revoked and its credentials removed at any time. Stored archives require separate deletion when they are no longer needed.

Third-party services and privacy

Google Drive, Railway, Better Stack and GitHub provide services used by this utility or these information pages. Their applicable terms also govern use of their services. The utility's data practices are described in its privacy policy.

Contact and changes

General questions can be raised in the Colvin repository. Keep private data, credentials and recovery material out of public issues. These terms may be updated as the utility changes; the current version and update date will remain available on this page.
