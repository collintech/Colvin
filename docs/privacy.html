Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy covers Colvin Scheduled Backups, the owner-operated utility maintained by collintech for Colvin staging operations. It covers the backup utility and its Google Drive authorization. The scheduled deployment is currently being configured and validated.

Information accessed and created

When enabled, the worker reads the Colvin staging database using a dedicated read-only role. A backup can contain personal information stored in that database. The worker encrypts the export with the operator's public recovery key before sending it to Google Drive.

Through Google's drive.file permission, the utility creates and manages its backup folder, encrypted archives, checksums and completion receipts. It reads file content and metadata as needed to verify uploads and manage completed backups. This permission covers files created by the app or explicitly authorized for it. The worker restricts its operations to its configured scheduled-backup folder.

How information is used

Google Drive data is used only to store, verify, identify and manage the operator's encrypted staging backups. OAuth tokens authorize those operations. Operational records can include timestamps, checksums, sizes, snapshot identifiers and source identifiers. A monitoring check-in is sent only after the backup workflow succeeds.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial-intelligence models. Colvin Scheduled Backups' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, access and service providers

  • Google Drive stores encrypted archives and their checksum and receipt files.
  • Railway runs the worker and stores its configured database reader credential and Google authorization configuration. The worker uses private temporary storage during each run.
  • Better Stack receives backup success check-ins for freshness monitoring.
  • The operator holds the private decryption key and its passphrase separately from the worker. Access to the destination folder is intended to remain owner-only.

These providers process information needed to deliver their respective services under their own terms and privacy policies. The worker is designed to keep credentials and plaintext database records out of its application logs.

Retention and deletion

Retention selects recent completed snapshots and additional weekly and monthly recovery points. Automatic cleanup starts disabled and requires validation before it is enabled. Cleanup is restricted to expired, recognized completed backups in the dedicated folder. Earlier manual backups are kept in a separate location.

The operator can stop the worker and remove its backup files from Google Drive. Files in provider trash, version history or provider-managed retention may require additional action or time before permanent removal. Revoking authorization alone does not delete existing archives.

Revoking Google access

The operator can revoke the utility's authorization in the Google Account third-party connections settings and remove the authorization configuration from Railway and the local computer. Revocation stops future authorized Drive operations and requires reauthorization before backups can resume.

This information website

These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They include no analytics scripts, sign-in forms or embedded third-party resources. GitHub may process ordinary website request information under its own privacy statement.

Contact and changes

General questions about this utility or this policy can be directed to the maintainer through the Colvin repository. Public issues must not include credentials, personal database records, archives or recovery keys. Changes to these practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
